# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

The primary audience is hiring managers, recruiters, clients, and cross-functional product stakeholders evaluating Yi-Ning Lo's ability to plan, define, and deliver digital systems.

## Product Purpose

This bilingual portfolio presents professional experience in system analysis, project management, product thinking, requirements planning, and delivery. Success means visitors can quickly understand the role, assess capabilities through project evidence, and reach out with confidence.

## Positioning

The portfolio demonstrates how complex business needs are translated into clear workflows, specifications, and executable product plans, rather than presenting a generic resume or a tool-focused design portfolio.

## Operating Context

Visitors scan the homepage for fit, explore selected project cases, read editorial case-study detail pages, switch between Traditional Chinese and English, and contact the owner. The owner maintains all primary content and imagery through the existing authenticated admin and Supabase CMS.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Preserve the existing public routes, project slugs, bilingual routing, Supabase data, authentication, CMS, storage, uploads, admin workflows, and data structures.
- Editable public content must continue to come from the CMS, with Chinese fallback when English content is absent.
- The public experience must work at mobile, tablet, desktop, and wide desktop sizes.
- The homepage information architecture remains Hero, About and Traits, Core Skills, Selected Projects, Contact, and Footer.
- Do not add KPI counters, work-process timelines, testimonials, pricing, a blog, a newsletter, or dashboard-style widgets.

## Brand Commitments

- Name and wordmark: 羅翊寧 Yi-Ning Lo / YL.
- Professional identity: Product Manager, System Analyst, and Product Thinker.
- Brand accent: deep ink green with calm light neutrals.
- Voice: clear, structured, mature, professional, and direct.
- Visual redesign direction: refined editorial product-studio composition with controlled richness, strong typography, large project imagery, disciplined metadata, subtle system-planning references, and restrained motion.

## Evidence on Hand

- Existing bilingual Hero, About, Traits, Skills, Projects, and Contact content in Supabase with a seed-data fallback.
- Existing portrait and project cover images, project metadata, Markdown case-study content, alt text, and contact information.
- Existing deployed public site, responsive components, SEO metadata, and Open Graph image.
- No testimonials, customer logos, performance claims, or verified numerical outcomes are available and none should be fabricated.

## Product Principles

1. Make complex professional work immediately understandable.
2. Let project evidence lead the experience.
3. Create richness through hierarchy, composition, imagery, and metadata instead of card chrome.
4. Preserve maintainability and CMS control while refining the public presentation.
5. Keep interaction purposeful, accessible, and calm.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Use semantic HTML, visible keyboard focus, descriptive image alternatives, sufficient color contrast, 44px touch targets, reduced-motion support, and layouts that remain readable without horizontal overflow.
